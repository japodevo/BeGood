FAQ

How does RainCheck find cheap Vancouver tickets?

We check the cheapest available seat (the floor price) for major Vancouver events every day and keep the full price history. Each event is scored against comparable events and its own recent prices, so you can see whether today's price is actually a deal.

Is the floor price the final price?

It's the cheapest listed ticket at our last daily refresh, before marketplace fees. Fees differ by marketplace — TickPick shows all-in pricing with no added fees, while others add fees at checkout — so compare a couple of marketplaces before buying.

How do the free alerts work?

Subscribe via the free ntfy app, the RSS feed, or our Telegram channel. When a tracked event's floor drops 10%+, the alert goes out automatically. No account, no email, no spam.

Does RainCheck sell tickets?

No — we're an independent price tracker and link out to established marketplaces. Some links are affiliate links, which may earn this site a commission at no extra cost to you.
